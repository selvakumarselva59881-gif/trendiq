System design

Pipeline

1 · CollectCSV, pasted data, APIs, social posts
→
2 · CleanParse numbers, drop blanks, validate length
→
3 · AnalyseMoving average, trend slope, z-score anomalies, sentiment lexicon
→
4 · ForecastHolt linear exponential smoothing with a widening range
→
5 · VisualiseCharts, KPIs, alerts and plain-language insights

Methods used in this prototype

Trend directionLeast-squares slope compared with the series average
ForecastHolt double exponential smoothing (level + trend)
AnomaliesPoints whose gap from the centred moving average exceeds k standard deviations
SentimentWord-list scoring with simple negation handling
